Reposition

Drag a turbine or the OSS — connected cables and their risk colours update live as you drag. Double-click a cable section to add a reroute point, then drag it independently. Turn on Move lock to freeze everything in place (e.g. before handing the mouse to a visitor). Positions and risk are recalculated against the real ground-conditions data for this patch.

Draw new cable

Click a turbine or the OSS to start a new cable, click empty water to add waypoints, then click another turbine/OSS to finish. Esc cancels. The route is sectioned and risk-coloured against the real ground data, and included in CAPEX & Programme automatically.

Click a cable section for full burial-depth detail, or a turbine for basic info.
